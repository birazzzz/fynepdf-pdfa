1. Overview

The year brought steady growth across all regions. Revenue rose by 18 percent, and the number of customers who use the service every week passed 40,000 for the first time.

“Keep it simple, and make it last.” This principle guided every decision this year.

1.1 Highlights

  • Three new products launched, including Smart Edit.
  • Support response time fell from 9 hours to 2.
  • The archive service now keeps every document for ten years.
