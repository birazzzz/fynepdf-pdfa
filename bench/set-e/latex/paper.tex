\section{Introduction}
PDF/A fixes a document's appearance: every font travels with the file, colours are defined,
and nothing depends on outside resources. For a document with $n$ pages and $f$ fonts, the
work of embedding is $O(n + f)$, but the gain lasts:
\begin{equation}
  \int_0^\infty e^{-\lambda t}\,\mathrm{d}t = \frac{1}{\lambda}, \qquad
  \sum_{k=1}^{n} k^2 = \frac{n(n+1)(2n+1)}{6}.
\end{equation}
